# Kodax — 15-second motion study

## Style Prompt
Use Kodax's current dark interface and purple identity, with the user's saved motion references: floating interface layers, deliberate perspective, a tap into the app icon, and continuous spatial handoffs. Tell one short story: choose the application, write a small Python snippet, publish it, and receive community reactions. Arabic copy is newly written for this demo. Format: 1920×1080, 15 seconds, 60 fps.

## Colors
- #0E0E14: app background, from src/design/tokens.ts.
- #16161F and #1D1D29: interface surfaces, from the same source.
- #8B5CF6 / #7C3AED: brand and pressed brand states.
- #EDEDF4 / #B6B6C8: primary and secondary text.
- #C4B5FD: bright purple brand text; #22C55E: successful execution.
- Syntax: #C792EA keywords, #A8D68A strings, #F78C6C numbers, #82AAFF functions.

## Typography
- IBM Plex Sans Arabic, using the application's local Regular, Medium, SemiBold and Bold TTF files.
- JetBrains Mono, using the application's local Regular TTF, for Python code only.
- Arabic words remain connected; reveal whole words or lines, never separate Arabic letters.

## Assets and evidence
- App icon: assets/icon.png.
- Ionicons glyph font and exact glyph names used by application navigation and SocialActions.
- Interface reference: assets/download-gallery/editor.png, src/features/community/SnippetCard.tsx, src/features/community/SocialActions.tsx and src/design/tokens.ts.
- Existing screenshots inspected: reports/design-polish/delivery/phone/home.png and community.png, reports/kodax-live-final-20261010/web/phone-code.png.
- The animated editor, demo post, counters and comment are illustrative reconstructions; no real post is published and no user account is needed.

## Motion
- 0–2.35s: app grid, pointer tap and icon response.
- 2.15–6.90s: iris reveal to the editor; code lines appear, execution succeeds, publish button is tapped.
- 6.65–11.55s: connected zoom/blur handoff into a community post; likes rise and a comment arrives.
- 11.25–14s: quiet brand close with three Arabic words and the real app icon.
- Revision 2: No music or harmonic bed. Discrete synchronized sound effects only: mechanical tap, filtered air sweeps, keyboard transients, publication impact and brief notification textures. Silence between actions.

## Revision 2 — user correction
- Replace the slide-like split layout entirely with a single continuous product choreography.
- V02: perspective phone reveal, camera approach, interface detached into foreground, reaction region enlarged.
- V05: carry the selected icon through a shape-changing transition; controls become the next scene.
- 0–2.2s: tilted phone and app grid, tap, push through selected icon.
- 2.2–5.4s: floating editor, close approach into code, run-result strip detaches.
- 5.4–8.3s: publish control expands; the SAME code panel becomes a community post.
- 8.3–11.7s: foreground reaction controls, close approach, typed comment and notifications.
- 11.7–15s: reverse camera pull and whole Arabic words, then icon/name resolution.
- Use strong depth changes, unequal layer timing, anticipation and overshoot. No decorative repeating dance or generic fade between unrelated slides.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not use Cairo from the older promo; the current app uses IBM Plex Sans Arabic.
- Do not copy wording, logos or soundtrack from the six reference videos.
- Do not claim demo engagement counters are real application statistics.
- Do not show QA usernames, test identifiers or credentials in the video.
- Do not turn the video into a slideshow of static screenshots.
