# Kodax motion identity

## Style Prompt
An energetic Arabic product film built from the supplied Kodax references: deep plum surfaces, electric violet accents, large confident Arabic typography, real app screens, and playful code symbols. Product UI stays sharp and readable. Motion turns learning into visible progress, then expands into competition and community. Portrait 1080 × 1920, 52 seconds, 30 fps. Sound effects only, without music or narration.

## Colors
- #100D20 — main canvas
- #201A38 — raised product surfaces
- #8525FA — brand violet from supplied references
- #C4A4FF — readable lavender accent
- #F7F2FF — primary text
- #C2B7D9 — secondary text
- #8AE0C2 — success and progress
- #FFD88A — earned experience and achievement

## Typography
Cairo variable font, 900 for headlines and 400 for supporting Arabic copy. Consolas monospace for code and numeric UI. Arabic is RTL; code and Kodax wordmark are LTR.

## Motion
Primary transition: horizontal push, 0.45s. Two circle reveals at chapter changes; a soft final dissolve. Staggered individual entrances, dynamic XP fill, podium growth, social notifications, code lines, certificate seal. Controlled drift and pulses on hero devices. Every scene gets at least three entrance eases. All motion is deterministic and seekable.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not invent download links, availability, accreditation, or real user statistics.
- Do not stretch screenshots or replace their Arabic UI.
- Do not use dense paragraphs or tiny mobile captions.
- Do not use generic stock footage or unrelated neon colors.
- Do not add music or voiceover; the user requested motion-synced sound effects only.

## Scene timing
0–6 hook and brand; 6–13 courses; 13–19 editor; 19–25 challenges and XP; 25–31 governorate/Iraq ranking; 31–38 code community; 38–43 assistant; 43–48 certificates; 48–52 closing.
